04 / Case Study

THOD × Flytime

When content meets experience.

Project
Flytime Pop-Up Fest — Eko Convention Centre
Role
Content Strategist · Campaign Planner · Marketer / Influencer · Content Creator · Video Editor
Background
THOD was invited to showcase and sell products at the Flytime Pop-Up Fest alongside other fashion brands.
the idea —

Fit Check Station

I proposed a full-length mirror where customers could try on their purchases, take photos and create organic, shareable content around the brand.

the idea —

Loyalty Cards

I proposed a "buy today, discount next time" system to encourage customers to return after the event.

My contribution also included additional marketing strategies, event content, promotional videos and recap/editing work.

Most important outcome
SOLD OUT.
04.1 / Deep Dive

Idea to sold out.

The Challenge

Standing out in a crowded room

A busy fashion pop-up brings dozens of brands into one space. The challenge was creating an experience — not just a stall — that encouraged people to interact, engage and ultimately buy.

My Thinking

Design for participation

  • The fit-check station turned trying-on into content people wanted to share themselves.
  • Loyalty cards extended the relationship beyond a single event day.
  • Content and marketing ideas kept the brand visible before, during and after.
Execution

On the ground, on camera

Event coverage, campaign ideas, editing and promotional material came together across the day — documenting the fest while actively driving people toward the booth.

Result

Sold out

THOD completely sold out during the event.
